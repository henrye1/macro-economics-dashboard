Macro data service

Annual macroeconomic data for every country, from two public sources, served from Cyte's own database.

IMF World Economic Outlook contributes realised history and staff forecasts about five years forward. World Bank WDI contributes realised history for a curated set of core indicators. Both are ingested on a schedule, so reads never touch the upstream sources.
